Travel Expenses made Easy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The short version

Travel Expenses made Easy collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. The app makes no network requests of its own — your receipts, the amounts read off them, and the trips you file them under never leave your device.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app keeps is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • The trips you create, and their names.
  • The receipt photos you scan and the PDFs you import, kept as files in the app's own container.
  • What was read off each receipt — merchant, amount, currency and date — along with the recognised text, so you can correct it later.

None of it is synced or uploaded by the app. Deleting the app removes all of it. If you have iCloud or iTunes device backups switched on, the app's data is included in those backups like any other app's, under Apple's privacy policy rather than this one.

How receipts are read

Scanning happens entirely on your device. Photographs go through Apple's on-device text recognition; a PDF that already carries a text layer is read directly from the file. The recognised text is then passed to Apple Intelligence's on-device language model to pick out the merchant and the total, and when Apple Intelligence is unavailable the app falls back to its own parsing rules.

No part of this involves a server. No image, no PDF and no recognised text is transmitted anywhere.

Network activity

The app contains no networking code. It does not call home, check for updates, or report usage. The only network activity associated with it is Apple's own: the App Store handles purchases and restores through StoreKit, which is a conversation between your device and Apple that the app never sees the contents of.

Purchases

Travel Expenses Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — the app never receives or stores your name, payment method, or Apple Account details. All it does is ask StoreKit whether a Pro purchase is currently active on the device and get back yes or no. There is no account to create and no purchase record held by us.

Exporting and sharing

When you export a trip, the app builds a zip of the receipt PDFs and a summary.csv and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there — AirDrop, Files, Mail, or anything else — is your choice, and what happens to it afterwards is governed by whatever app or service you send it to.

Sharing a photo or PDF into the app from Photos, Mail or Files works the same way in reverse: the file is copied into the app's own storage and nothing else is read.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph paper receipts with the document scanner. Images are written straight into the app's storage.
  • Photo library — only the photos you explicitly pick are read.
  • Files — only the documents you explicitly pick are read.

The app does not ask for your location, your contacts, your calendar, or your health data, and it has no use for them.

Tracking and third parties

There is none. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no third-party code of any kind beyond Apple's own frameworks. Nothing tracks you across apps or websites, and the app never asks for permission to, because it has nothing to ask for.

This web page is a different matter: like the rest of this site, it uses a privacy-focused analytics script to count page views. That applies to your visit here, not to the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. Your own copy is on your device, yours to edit, export or delete at any time.

Changes
