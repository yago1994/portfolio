Download Broom for free

Most of the internet was designed to capture your attention, not to protect it. We navigate digital spaces filled with noise — sidebars, "premium" nudges, and infinite recommendations — that we never actually asked for.

I built Broom to change the power dynamic of the browser. Instead of accepting the UI you're given, Broom lets you curate the UI you need. It's a tool for reclaiming your focus by stripping away the digital clutter and replacing it with something that actually brings you peace (in my case, a growing garden of plants).

The web shouldn't be something you consume — it should be a space that works for you.
