Privacy

What Remou can see

Remou is a Safari extension that runs entirely inside your browser. It reads the content of Outlook Web pages in order to restructure email threads into a chat-like layout. This processing happens locally on your device — no email content is ever sent anywhere.

Data storage

Remou stores only your preferences (for example, whether the extension is enabled) using Safari's standard extension storage API. This data never leaves your device.

Network activity

Remou makes no network requests of its own. It does not connect to any external server, collect analytics, or transmit any information about you or your emails.

Bug reporting

If you choose to report a bug, diagnostic information about your browser environment and the extension's state may be shared in order to help diagnose the issue. This only happens when you explicitly initiate a bug report — nothing is sent automatically.

Third parties

Remou does not share any data with third parties except as described above (bug reporting). There are no ads, no trackers, and no analytics SDKs included in the extension.
